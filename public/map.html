☦ Orthodox Jurisdiction Map
Explore the geographic structure and boundaries of Orthodox parishes, dioceses, and patriarchates across North America
🗺️ What This Map Shows
This interactive map displays the territorial jurisdiction of Orthodox Christian parishes across North America. Each colored region represents a diocese or jurisdiction, and you can explore parish locations, boundaries, diocesan organization, and hierarchical relationships.
🎮 How to Navigate
🖱️
Pan & Zoom: Click and drag to pan, use scroll wheel to zoom in/out
👆
Select: Click on a diocese boundary or parish marker to view details
🔍
Search: Use / to focus the search box, or type directly
⚙️ Controls & Filters
Search Find parishes by name, location, or diocese
Filters Filter by diocese, patriarchate, state, or importance (Cathedral, Monastery, etc.)
Visualization Toggle diocese boundaries, parish territories, markers, and labels
Statistics View counts of visible parishes, dioceses, and clustering statistics
💡 Pro Tip Click on the legend (left side) to show/hide entire jurisdictions at once. Use the controls panel on the right to fine-tune what you're viewing.
